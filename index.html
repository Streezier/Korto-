Meet the founder

Ayush Jha · Founder, Korto

I started my journey very young, at just 15, with big dreams and very little experience. I had no big budget or team, only curiosity and the belief that online shopping could be simpler and more honest.

When my first order came in, I was actually at a loss of Rs. 1000. It would have been easy to quit. Instead, I kept learning, kept improving, and the orders started coming in more and more. Step by step, that grew into Korto.

"Every order is a person trusting us. My job is to earn that trust again and again."

- Ayush Jha
Founder, Korto
